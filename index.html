About Us

Oon Jai Marketplace is a mission-driven vegetarian and vegan restaurant located in Vientiane, Laos. More than just a place to enjoy wholesome food, Oon Jai exists to promote healthy living, compassionate service, and meaningful relationships.

Every meal served represents an opportunity to share kindness, care, and connection — nourishing not only the body but also the community.
