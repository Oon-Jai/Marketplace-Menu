About Us

Oon Jai Marketplace is a mission-driven vegetarian and vegan restaurant located in Vientiane, Laos. More than just a place to enjoy wholesome food, Oon Jai exists to promote healthy living, compassionate service, and meaningful relationships through plant-based meals and community engagement.

Every meal served and every person welcomed represents an opportunity to share kindness, care, and connection. Through food and hospitality, Oon Jai seeks to demonstrate that a simple meal can become a powerful expression of love, compassion, and service—nourishing not only the body but also the community.

For more information about Oon Jai and our mission, please visit us at www.oonjai.org
